## Reusable lessons promoted

- Improve observability before declaring a device or component failed.
- Use discriminating controls to narrow a failure family without inventing the
  exact failed part.
- Keep quick diagnostic PASS bounded to its declared coverage.
- Separate recovered access, workaround, physical repair and fitness-for-use.
- Separate owner-reported, observed and derived evidence.
- Separate data recovery from media reliability.
- Require compatibility, functional proof and economic rationale before donor reuse.
- Treat current health as distinct from durable recovery.
